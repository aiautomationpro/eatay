🍳 Performance: Naturally Non-Stick Without the Chemicals

One of the biggest reasons people choose Caraway is the ceramic non-stick surface.

Unlike traditional non-stick cookware that contains PTFE, PFOA, or PFAS, this set is:

✔ PFAS-free
✔ PFOA-free
✔ PTFE-free
✔ Lead & cadmium free

The slick ceramic coating allows food to release easily using minimal oil — perfect for eggs, pancakes, fish, and delicate sauces.

Real Benefit:
Less oil → healthier meals → easier cleanup.

Most messes wipe away with a soft sponge and warm water.

🔥 Stovetop & Oven Safe (Up to 550°F)

Versatility matters in cookware. This set works with:

  • Induction cooktops

  • Gas stoves

  • Electric ranges

Plus, it's oven safe up to 550°F, so you can easily transfer dishes from stovetop to oven without switching pans.

Great for:

  • Searing then roasting meats

  • Baked pasta dishes

  • One-pan dinners

🧼 Cleaning & Maintenance

Cleaning is refreshingly simple.

Because of the ceramic surface:

  • Food doesn’t stick easily

  • No aggressive scrubbing needed

  • Hand washing is quick and effortless

💡 Tip: Avoid metal utensils and high heat to extend the life of the ceramic coating.
