Asian Raw Carrot Salad Recipe

By Mia Gomez
February 23, 2026

Craving a fresh, vibrant salad that’s packed with flavor and crunch? My Asian Raw Carrot Salad is a perfect choice — tangy, slightly sweet, and spicy, it’s a refreshing side dish or light lunch that everyone will love. The ribbons of raw carrot coated in a zesty dressing, with toasted sesame seeds and optional herbs or nuts, make every bite a burst of color and taste. Whether you’re a busy home cook or a meal prep enthusiast, this salad is quick to make and always satisfying.

Ingredients List

Salad Ingredients

  • 4 medium carrots, shaved into ribbons

  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar

  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce (or tamari for gluten-free)

  • 1–2 teaspoons honey or maple syrup (for vegan)

  • 1 teaspoon chili flakes (adjust to taste)

  • 2.5 tablespoons olive oil

  • 1 tablespoon toasted sesame seeds

  • A splash of lime or lemon juice

  • Salt to taste

Optional Garnishes

  • Thinly sliced scallions

  • Fresh herbs (mint, cilantro, Thai basil)

  • Crushed peanuts or toasted cashews

Required Kitchen Tools

Making this salad is simple, but having the right tools makes prep effortless:

  1. Vegetable Peeler – To create beautiful carrot ribbons that hold the dressing well.

  2. Mixing Bowl – A medium bowl to toss all the ingredients evenly.

  3. Whisk or Fork – For combining the dressing ingredients smoothly.

  4. Measuring Spoons – Ensures perfect balance of sweet, salty, and tangy flavors.

With these tools, preparing this refreshing Asian carrot salad is quick and enjoyable.

Salad Preparation

Preparing this salad is straightforward and doesn’t require cooking — perfect for a fresh, raw dish.

Making the Dressing

In a small bowl, whisk together rice vinegar, soy sauce or tamari, honey or maple syrup, olive oil, chili flakes, lime or lemon juice, and a pinch of salt. Taste and adjust sweetness or acidity as needed. This tangy, slightly sweet dressing is the flavor backbone of the salad.

Tossing the Salad

Place your carrot ribbons in a large mixing bowl. Pour the dressing over the carrots and toss gently to coat each ribbon evenly. Sprinkle the toasted sesame seeds on top and toss lightly again. The sesame seeds add a subtle nutty flavor and a delicate crunch.

Garnishing and Serving

I love to elevate this salad with optional garnishes:

  • Scatter thinly sliced scallions or fresh herbs like mint, cilantro, or Thai basil for a fresh aroma.

  • Add crushed peanuts or toasted cashews for extra crunch and richness.

Serve immediately for the freshest taste, or refrigerate for up to 2 hours to allow the flavors to meld. This salad pairs beautifully with grilled meats, tofu, or as a vibrant side for any meal.

Storage Instructions

Keeping the Salad Fresh

This salad is best enjoyed fresh. If needed, store it in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 day. The carrots will maintain their crunch, but the herbs are best added just before serving.

Tips for Meal Prep

If you plan to meal prep, keep the dressing separate and toss just before eating to retain the crisp texture of the carrot ribbons.

Conclusion

This Asian Raw Carrot Salad is a simple yet flavor-packed dish that’s as beautiful as it is delicious. The combination of tangy, sweet, and spicy flavors, enhanced with sesame seeds, herbs, and optional nuts, makes it irresistible. It’s perfect for a quick lunch, side dish, or an elegant starter for dinner parties. With minimal prep and maximum flavor, this salad has quickly become one of my favorite go-to recipes for fresh, healthy, and vibrant meals.
