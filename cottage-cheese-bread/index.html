Easy cottage cheese bread Recipe

By Mia Gomez
February 14, 2026

Craving a soft, moist, and slightly tangy bread that's perfect for breakfast, snacks, or even a light dinner? My Easy Cottage Cheese Bread is exactly what you need. This simple, no-fuss bread combines the creaminess of cottage cheese with the fluffiness of a homemade loaf, creating a tender texture that melts in your mouth. Whether you’re a beginner baker or just looking for a quick bread recipe, this loaf delivers delicious results every time.

Ingredients List

Dry Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour

  • 1 1/2 tablespoons baking powder

  • 1 1/4 teaspoons salt

Wet Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups milk (any type)

  • 1 cup cottage cheese (I used 2%)

Required Kitchen Tools

Having the right tools makes baking this bread effortless. Here’s what I use:

  1. Mixing Bowl – A large bowl is perfect for combining dry and wet ingredients without spilling.

  2. Whisk or Spoon – For mixing the batter smoothly until just combined.

  3. Loaf Pan – Standard 9x5-inch loaf pan works best for even baking.

  4. Oven Thermometer – To ensure your oven reaches the correct temperature for a perfect bake.

  5. Cooling Rack – Essential for letting the bread cool completely, avoiding soggy bottoms.

With these tools, making this cottage cheese bread is simple and stress-free.

Bread Preparation

Making this bread is a breeze, and the steps are straightforward even for beginner bakers.

Mixing the Ingredients

First, I combine the dry ingredients—flour, baking powder, and salt—in a large mixing bowl. In a separate bowl, I whisk together the milk and cottage cheese until smooth. Then, I gently fold the wet mixture into the dry ingredients, stirring until just combined. I make sure not to overmix; the batter should be slightly lumpy, which keeps the bread light and fluffy.

Preparing for Baking

Once the batter is ready, I grease my loaf pan with butter or a light spray of oil. I pour the batter evenly into the pan, smoothing the top with a spatula. Then, I let it rest for a few minutes while the oven preheats to 350°F (175°C).

Baking the Bread

I bake the loaf in a preheated oven for 50–60 minutes, checking for doneness by inserting a toothpick in the center—it should come out clean. The top should be lightly golden brown and firm to the touch. Once baked, I carefully remove the bread from the oven and let it cool in the pan for 10 minutes before transferring it to a cooling rack to cool completely.

Serving and Enjoying

This cottage cheese bread is wonderfully versatile. I love slicing it warm and spreading a little butter or jam for breakfast. It also pairs perfectly with soups, salads, or even as a base for a savory sandwich. The slight tang from the cottage cheese gives it a unique flavor that keeps everyone coming back for more.

Suggested Variations

  • Herb Infusion: Add 1–2 teaspoons of dried herbs like rosemary or thyme to the dry ingredients for a savory twist.

  • Cheese Lover’s Delight: Mix in 1/2 cup grated cheddar or mozzarella for extra cheesiness.

  • Sweet Version: Stir in 2 tablespoons of honey or maple syrup and a handful of raisins for a subtle sweet bread.

Storage Instructions

Keeping the Bread Fresh

I store leftover bread in an airtight container at room temperature for 2–3 days. To maintain softness, I wrap it in a clean kitchen towel or parchment paper before sealing.

Freezing the Bread

For longer storage, I slice the bread and place the slices in a freezer-safe bag. It keeps well for up to 2 months. To enjoy, I simply thaw slices at room temperature or toast them lightly—it tastes just as fresh as the day I baked it.

Conclusion

Making Easy Cottage Cheese Bread has become one of my favorite baking projects. The combination of simplicity, minimal ingredients, and irresistible texture makes it perfect for busy mornings or casual baking days. With just a few steps, a loaf of soft, moist, and flavorful bread comes out of your oven ready to enjoy. Whether you stick to the classic version or try creative variations, this recipe guarantees a delicious homemade bread experience every time.
